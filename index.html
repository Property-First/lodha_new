About Lodha Fiorana

Lodha Fiorana at Beaumont Estate Project Overview

Lodha Fiorana at Beaumont Estate is a luxury residential development by Lodha Group, located in Sadahalli, North Bangalore. The project offers spacious 3 & 4 BHK residences within a thoughtfully planned, low-density residential environment.

The development is designed around landscaped open spaces, nature, wellness and a neo-classical architectural character. The project material highlights approximately 85% open space, a 2-acre clubhouse, lake-facing surroundings and limited residences per cluster as key elements of the community.

Located near Kempegowda International Airport, Lodha Fiorana provides convenient access to the airport corridor, Doddajala Metro Station, NH-44 / Bellary Road and other key destinations across Devanahalli and North Bangalore.
