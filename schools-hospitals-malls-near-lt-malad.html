Schools, Hospitals & Malls Near L&T Malad

August 06, 2026 5 min read Location, Schools, Hospitals, Malls, Neighborhood

When you buy a home, you are not just buying four walls — you are buying an entire neighborhood. The best home in the world feels incomplete if there is no good school nearby for your children, no hospital for emergencies, and no mall for weekend fun. That is why the location of L&T Malad is so special.

L&T Malad is located in Malad East, one of the most well-connected areas in Mumbai. In this blog, we will look at the best schools, hospitals, and malls close to this project. We will keep everything in simple words — like a class project about "why location matters."

1. Top Schools Near L&T Malad

Education is the foundation of a good life. Parents always want the best schools for their children. Luckily, L&T Malad is surrounded by some of the most respected schools in Mumbai. These schools are just a short drive away, which means your children can spend less time travelling and more time learning.

Here are some of the well-known schools nearby:

  • Ryan International School – Famous for its modern teaching methods and excellent sports facilities.
  • DAV Public School – A top school with a strong focus on academics and values.
  • Holy Family High School – Known for its caring teachers and good results.
  • N.G. Acharya & D.K. Marathe College – A great option for higher secondary education.

Most of these schools are located within 5 to 10 minutes from the project. This means your children can walk or take a short school bus ride. No more waking up at 5 AM to catch a long train!

2. Best Hospitals and Healthcare Facilities

Health is wealth. When you or your family members need medical help, you want to reach the hospital quickly. L&T Malad is close to some of the finest hospitals and clinics in Mumbai.

Here are the top medical facilities nearby:

  • HCG Hospital – A multi-specialty hospital with experienced doctors and advanced technology.
  • Reliance Hospital – Part of the Reliance Group, this hospital offers world-class treatment.
  • Kaiser Hospital – Known for its emergency care and friendly staff.
  • Many local clinics and nursing homes – For everyday health check-ups and minor issues.

Having these hospitals nearby gives you peace of mind. In case of an emergency, you can reach the hospital in just 5 to 10 minutes. This is one of the biggest advantages of living in a well-connected area like Malad East.

3. Shopping Malls and Entertainment Hubs

Life is not just about work and school — it is also about having fun! On weekends, families love to go out for shopping, watching movies, or eating at restaurants. L&T Malad is surrounded by some amazing malls and entertainment zones.

Here are the popular shopping destinations nearby:

  • Inorbit Mall – One of the biggest malls in the area. You can shop for clothes, electronics, and groceries. It also has a great food court and a cinema hall.
  • Mindspace Mall – A modern mall with many international brands and a fun gaming zone for children.
  • Growel's 101 Mall – Located in nearby Kandivali, this mall has everything from clothing stores to a huge supermarket.
  • Local Markets – For daily needs like vegetables, fruits, and fresh flowers, there are many local markets just a short walk away.

These malls are all within 10 to 15 minutes from L&T Malad. You can go for a quick shopping trip or plan a full day of entertainment with your family.

4. Great Connectivity – Everything is Close!

The best part about this location is the connectivity. The Western Express Highway is very close, which connects you to the airport, railway stations, and other parts of Mumbai. This means you can reach your office, your child's school, and the hospital without getting stuck in traffic for hours.

When you live at L&T Malad, you save so much time that you can spend more hours with your family. Isn't that the best gift you can give yourself?
