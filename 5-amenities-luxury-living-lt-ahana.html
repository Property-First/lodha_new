5 Amenities That Define Luxury Living at L&T Ahana

August 05, 2026 6 min read Luxury Living · Amenities

L&T Ahana Malad is redefining luxury living in Mumbai. Nestled in one of the city's most promising corridors, this residential masterpiece by L&T Realty brings together world-class design, thoughtful planning, and an array of amenities that elevate everyday living. What truly sets L&T Ahana apart is the meticulous attention to detail — from wellness to entertainment, security to sustainability.

Whether you're a homebuyer seeking the perfect family nest or an investor eyeing long-term appreciation, the amenities at L&T Ahana are designed to deliver a lifestyle that's both indulgent and practical. Let's walk through the five standout features that define luxury living at this landmark project.

1. Infinity Pool with Panoramic Views

Imagine starting your day with a refreshing swim while soaking in breathtaking views of the Mumbai skyline. The infinity-edge swimming pool at L&T Ahana is more than just a pool — it's a retreat. Designed with a sleek, modern aesthetic, it offers a seamless visual connection to the horizon. Whether you're doing laps or simply lounging by the deck, this amenity transforms your daily routine into a resort-like experience. The pool area is complemented by a sunken lounge and cabanas, perfect for weekend relaxation or hosting intimate gatherings.

2. State-of-the-Art Fitness & Wellness Centre

Health and wellness are at the core of L&T Ahana's design philosophy. The project features a fully equipped, spa-inspired fitness centre with cutting-edge cardio and strength-training equipment. But it doesn't stop there — residents also have access to a dedicated yoga studio, a meditation deck, and a steam room for post-workout recovery. The fitness centre is designed by experts to cater to all age groups, from young professionals to senior citizens. Additionally, a jogging track meanders through lush landscaping, making outdoor exercise a pleasure.

3. Landscaped Gardens & Open Spaces

In a city where green spaces are a luxury, L&T Ahana delivers with expansive landscaped gardens and open recreational areas. The project features a central green piazza, a children's adventure park, and multiple sensory gardens designed for relaxation and rejuvenation. Every corner of the outdoor space is thoughtfully curated with native flora, water features, and seating nooks. Whether you're taking a morning stroll, reading a book under a tree, or watching your children play, these open spaces offer a much-needed escape from the city's hustle.

4. Premium Clubhouse with Indoor & Outdoor Recreation

The grand clubhouse at L&T Ahana is the social heart of the community. Spread across multiple levels, it offers a host of recreational facilities:

  • Indoor sports – badminton, table tennis, and squash courts
  • Outdoor sports – tennis court, basketball half-court, and a putting green
  • Entertainment zone – a private theatre, game room, and party hall
  • Co-working lounge – for residents who work from home
  • Library & reading room – a quiet space for book lovers

The clubhouse is designed to foster a sense of community, with spaces for both active recreation and quiet reflection.

5. Smart Home Automation & 360° Security

Luxury in the modern era is incomplete without smart technology. L&T Ahana comes equipped with a comprehensive home automation system that allows residents to control lighting, climate, and appliances via a mobile app. But that's just the beginning. The project features a multi-layered security framework including:

  • 24/7 CCTV surveillance with facial recognition
  • Video door phones with remote access
  • Smart access control at all entry points
  • Fire safety systems with automated alerts
  • Emergency response protocols integrated with local services

This ensures that your family's safety is never compromised, giving you complete peace of mind.
